Beef Noodles Recipe

Description

This is a delicious recipe for beef noodles, it's one of Kateryna's most perfect cooking creations.

Cooking time : 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • Viande hachée
  • Nouilles de riz (glass noodles)
  • Oignons
  • 1 Oignon rouge
  • Carottes
  • Poivrons
  • Gingembre
  • 2 gousses d'ail
  • Sauce soja
  • Huile de sésame
  • 2 cuillères à soupe de graines de sésame

Steps

  1. Cook the glass noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. In a large pan, heat some oil and sauté the onions, carrots, and bell peppers until they are tender.
  3. Add the minced garlic and ginger, and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the ground beef to the pan and cook until browned.
  5. Pour in the soy sauce and sesame oil, and stir to combine all the ingredients.
  6. Add the cooked glass noodles to the pan and toss everything together until well mixed.
  7. Sprinkle with sesame seeds before serving.

Pizza Recipe

Description

This is the best recipe in the world for home made pizza.

Ingredients

  • Farine
  • Eau
  • levure
  • huile d'olive
  • sel
  • poolish

Steps

  1. Make the poolish first
  2. wait 24 hours (poolish in the fridge)
  3. Make the pizza dough with 70% hydration
  4. Let the dough rest for 2 hours
  5. Work the dough carefully and shape the pizza
  6. Add quickly the homemade tomato sauce
  7. Add the mozzarella cheese
  8. Add the desired toppings (don't overdo it!)
